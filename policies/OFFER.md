# Offer: Leadfeeder Demo

**Policy ID**: `OFFER_DEMO_V1`

## Product

Leadfeeder helps B2B revenue teams identify companies visiting their website
and understand the commercial context of that activity.

## Buyer Problem

Relevant companies often research a product or service without submitting a
form. Sales and marketing teams can miss those signals, lack enough context to
judge them quickly, or react after the moment has passed.

## Value

Leadfeeder gives the team a clearer view of visiting accounts so people can
decide where attention is warranted. It supports better-informed review and
follow-up; it does not promise that every visit signals active buying intent.

## Desired Outcome

A revenue team can use timely website context to make a thoughtful human
decision: investigate a potentially relevant account, wait for more context, or
leave it alone. Consumer-only analytics and automatic mass outreach are outside
this offer.

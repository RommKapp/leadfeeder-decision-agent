# Human Follow-Up Principles

**Policy ID**: `OUTREACH_DEMO_V1`

## Purpose

The agent's result helps a person decide whether and how to look into an
account. It does not send outreach, select a personal contact, write a message,
or change an external system.

## Principles

- Treat website activity as context for human judgment, not proof of intent.
- Review a promising account before choosing a channel, message, or owner.
- Use the available business context to make any eventual follow-up relevant
  and respectful.
- When the signal is incomplete or ambiguous, prefer waiting for better context
  or checking the data over forcing a sales action.
- Leave clearly unsuitable accounts alone.

## Boundary

This policy describes responsible human behaviour after an analysis. The
Recommendation skill owns the operational decision, ordering, and suggested
human action used by the workflow.

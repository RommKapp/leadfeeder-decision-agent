# Ideal Customer Profile

**Policy ID**: `ICP_DEMO_V1`
**Revision**: `3`

## Target Company

The strongest fit is a B2B company with a meaningful website-led buyer journey:
prospective customers use its site to research a product or service before, or
alongside, a conversation with the revenue team.

It is typically a mid-market organisation, roughly 50 to 2,000 employees, in
Europe or North America. This is large enough to have a repeatable sales or
marketing motion and a real need to focus attention, while still being a
practical operating environment for account-level follow-up.

The company sells to other businesses rather than primarily to consumers. Its
sales and marketing teams benefit when they can see meaningful account activity
on commercially relevant parts of the website, add that context to their own
judgment, and decide whether a person should investigate further.

## Why This Matters

Leadfeeder is most valuable when anonymous website activity can become useful
context for a revenue team. The goal is not to treat every visit as buying
intent or to replace human judgment. It is to help people notice potentially
relevant accounts while the signal is still timely, distinguish them from poor
fits, and spend attention where it may lead to a better customer conversation.

## Boundary

This policy describes the business meaning of an attractive company. The
project's ICP Scoring skill owns how available evidence is interpreted against
that description.
